Questions Answered

The honest answers, upfront.

Do you handle everything, or do I need separate architects and contractors?+
End-to-end. From land assessment and design to solar, water, landscaping, security and handover — one team, one accountable partner.
I already own land. Can you build a farmhouse on it?+
Absolutely. Whether you own a half acre or five acres, we'll assess the site and design a sustainable farmhouse that fits the land and your lifestyle.
I'm still planning to buy land. Can you help me with that too?+
Yes. We help you evaluate plots near growing corridors, check infrastructure feasibility, and avoid common buying mistakes before you commit.
How does the rental income work when I'm not using the farmhouse?+
Sustainable retreats are in high demand on stay platforms. We'll guide you on positioning your property so it can earn passively when it's vacant — completely optional.
How long does a farmhouse take to build?+
Typically 9–14 months end-to-end, depending on size and customization. We share a clear timeline at the proposal stage and stick to it.
Is sustainable construction more expensive?+
Slightly higher upfront, significantly cheaper to run. Solar, rainwater and bioclimatic design pay for themselves in years — and they make daily living healthier from day one.
